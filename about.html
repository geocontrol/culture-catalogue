About and privacy

What this is

Culture Catalogue is a directory of creative people and places. It doesn’t hold anyone’s data. Every entry is a CultureBlocs record that a person or place published in their own ATProto account. Culture Catalogue finds those records on the open network and shows them. Any other app can read the same records.

What you publish

Everything you publish here is public, like a post. People publish their name, what they do, and the area they are based in: a town, neighbourhood or borough, never an address. Places publish their address, because they are open to visit or book. Offers are public too, so they carry links, not email addresses or phone numbers.

What this site keeps

Nothing on a server. When you sign in, the sign-in session and its key are kept in your own browser so you can publish. Signing out removes them. Culture Catalogue writes only the records you publish here, and only to your own account. It uses no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Changing or removing your entry

Edit it from Add yourself, or from any app that reads CultureBlocs records. To remove it, delete the record from your account. If something listed here shouldn’t be, contact the catalogue and it will be hidden from this directory. The record itself stays in its owner’s account.
